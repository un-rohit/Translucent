Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Translucent (Rohit Indsutries)

Welcome to Translucent. Your privacy and trust are our highest priorities. This Privacy Policy describes how Translucent collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our Windows desktop application and related services.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Translucent is built with a privacy-first, stealth architecture:

  • No Keystroke Logging: We never log, store, or monitor your keystrokes. Global hotkeys are processed locally by your operating system solely to toggle app visibility.
  • No Background Screen Tracking: Translucent never monitors or records your screen in the background. Screen capture is initiated strictly on-demand by the user via the Snip tool.
  • No Personal Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any third party or advertising broker.

2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

All core settings and preferences are stored locally in secure app storage on your computer:

  • User interface preferences (transparency level, hotkey combinations, overlay sizes).
  • Local browser tab caches and cookies (managed securely within Microsoft WebView2).
  • Local chat history (stored locally on your disk and never transferred to third-party databases without your consent).

3. Artificial Intelligence & Cloud Services

AI Prompt Processing:

When you interact with the AI assistant or analyze a screen snip, your specific prompt text or snippet image is securely transmitted via encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3) directly to the AI service provider API (such as Google Gemini). This data is processed in real time to generate responses and is not retained for unauthorized profiling.

4. Windows Permissions & Capabilities

Translucent declares the runFullTrust capability in its Windows App package. This capability is required strictly to:

  • Render non-intrusive acrylic desktop overlay windows over other applications.
  • Register user-configured keyboard shortcuts (hotkeys) to show/hide the assistant.
  • Host the embedded browser container powered by Microsoft Edge WebView2.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (TLS/SSL) for all network communication between the application, your server, and third-party APIs. Access tokens and configuration keys are stored in encrypted format.

6. Children's Privacy

Translucent does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
